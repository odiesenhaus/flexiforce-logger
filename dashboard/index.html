Sensor calibration
–

  1. Condition it (recommended). Put the 500 g weight on and take it off 3–5 times, about 10 s each.
  2. Set the sensitivity. With 500 g on, turn this sensor's potentiometer until the reading just reaches the top, about 4.9–5.0 V. If you turn it again later, recalibrate.
  3. Record the weights. Start with nothing on the sensor. Place each weight the same way each time, wait about 10 s for the reading to settle, then capture it.
    g

  4. Save and check. Save the fit, put the weights back on, and check that the reading above is roughly right. Then go to the next sensor.
